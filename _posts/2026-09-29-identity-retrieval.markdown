We test whether dense retrievers respond to identity signals in queries (political ideology and dialect) across political news and health domains. Results: all five retrievers favor documents matching the query's political lean and retrieve worse results for African American Language queries than for White Mainstream English, and these biases are encoded deep in the query embeddings rather than in surface vocabulary.
