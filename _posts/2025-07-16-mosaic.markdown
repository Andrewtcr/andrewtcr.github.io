We measure the mosaic effect: whether sensitive facts can be rebuilt by aggregating individually less-sensitive documents, or recovered from an LLM's own memory. We build retrieval pipelines that audit which facts in real declassified government records are already exposed, and that propose and verify redactions for documents under release review.
